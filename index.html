Jieli Liu

PhD Candidate in Information Science at Indiana University Bloomington

Hello! I am Jieli Liu; my Chinese name is 刘洁丽. A quick pronunciation tip: by my admittedly informal estimate, pronouncing “Jieli” like “Jelly” achieves roughly 90% accuracy.

I earned a Bachelor of Management in Library and Information Science from Sun Yat-sen University in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. My thesis, The European Union Digital Competence Framework for Citizens: Origin, Development, and Implementation Research, was supervised by Dr. Qiong Tang and received Sun Yat-sen University’s Best Bachelor’s Thesis Award in 2018.

I earned a Master of Library and Information Science from Peking University in Beijing, China. There, I worked with Dr. Pengyi Zhang on knowledge sharing, online communities, and user studies.

I am currently a PhD candidate in Information Science at Indiana University Bloomington, advised by Dr. Pnina Fichman. My research spans social informatics and library and information science. My dissertation examines organizational generative AI adoption in leading academic libraries, with a particular focus on the factors influencing adoption within technological, organizational, and environmental contexts. My work will contribute to a better understanding of how academic libraries adopt emerging technologies and shed light on how they can adopt generative AI responsibly.

I am currently on the job market, seeking research roles in academia or industry and academic librarian positions. I welcome conversations about my research, teaching, or other opportunities for collaboration. The best way to reach me is by email at liujiel@iu.edu.
