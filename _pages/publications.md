My research examines how libraries respond to emerging technologies while maintaining their commitments to equitable access, high-quality information services, and responsible technology adoption and governance. 

I have also published published research based on course projects related to healthy information environments, examining how science communication can help mitigate misinformation, and the factors influencing online community engagement and sustanability.
